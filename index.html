FAQs

How does URL shortening work?

URL shortening works by taking a long URL and creating a shorter, condensed version that redirects to the original URL. When a user clicks on the shortened link, they are redirected to the intended destination.

Is it necessary to create an account to use the URL shortening service?

You can start shortening links quickly, but an account gives you access to features such as custom URLs, QR codes and link analytics.

Are the shortened links permanent? Will they expire?

Your shortened links stay active for as long as your account and plan remain active. Details on expiry are listed with each plan.

Are there any limitations on the number of URLs I can shorten?

The Basic plan lets you shorten unlimited URLs. Paid plans add extra features on top, rather than more links.

Can I customize the shortened URLs to reflect my brand or content?

Yes. You can create custom slugs and branded links, and use your own domain customization options to match your brand.

Can I track the performance of my shortened URLs?

Yes. Link analytics show how your shortened URLs, custom URLs and QR codes are being used, so you can monitor progress.

How secure is the URL shortening service? Are the shortened links protected against spam or malicious activity?

We take security seriously. Links are monitored for spam and malicious activity, and paid plans add enhanced security features.

What is a QR code and what can it do?

A QR code is a scannable image that opens a link when a phone camera points at it. Use it to bring your audience from print, packaging or events straight to your page.

Is there an API available for integrating the URL shortening service into my own applications or websites?

API access is available on the Teams plan, so you can connect URL shortening to your own applications and websites.
